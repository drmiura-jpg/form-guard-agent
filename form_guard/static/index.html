Human-in-the-loop by design. AI output is a draft. Every value shows its verbatim source; a person confirms each field before the file can be downloaded.

人が全項目を確認するまで、ダウンロードはサーバー側でロックされます。

Fill the form. Prove nothing else changed.

Generative AI that re-writes a Word or Excel form tends to break borders, merged cells and legal text, and to invent numbers. Form-Guard writes only into the designated fields, byte-for-byte, and shows a source for every value.

  1. Scanfind {{markers}} and printed rules (字数・和暦・上限)
  2. Map & self-correctClaude or the offline engine, verbatim quotes only
  3. Surgical writereplace marker characters inside the XML, nothing else
  4. VerifyZIP · XML skeleton · tables · borders · text · grounding
  5. Human sign-offchecklist, then the download unlocks
